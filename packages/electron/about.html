Nimbalyst is the visual workspace for building with Codex and Claude Code

  • Agentic WYSIWYG visual editors for markdown, mockups, excalidraw, mermaid, data models, csv and code. Extensions.
  • Parallel session management and kanban to understand session state, search and resume sessions, and link sessions to files and files to sessions
  • Item tracking and kanban to manage plans, bugs, and tasks with your agent
  • Open storage of content and status in plain files on disk and git.
